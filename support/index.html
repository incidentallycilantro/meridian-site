Common questions

Why is my schedule empty?

Meridian stays empty until you grant calendar access and turn on the calendars you want under Accounts & Calendars. Events remain in Apple Calendar / your providers.

Does Meridian upload my calendar?

No. Event titles, notes, locations, and invitees stay on device. Optional Help Improve Meridian (default off) sends only anonymous feature counters — never schedule content.

Can I create and edit events?

Yes. Meridian can create, edit, and delete events in calendars you allow, through Apple EventKit. Choose the destination calendar when creating when more than one writable calendar is available.

Why don’t I see leave-by times?

Alpha builds do not guess drive times from your current location. If an event has a place, use Directions to open Apple Maps.

Notifications

Optional. Point-of-use prompts only. Bodies do not include calendar notes.

Photos

Parse can pick a photo via the system picker for OCR. Meridian does not require full Photos library access.

Delete Improve data

Settings → Privacy → delete Improve data from Meridian servers (when Improve was ever enabled).

Device requirements

iPhone (primary). iPad may run with basic schedule correctness; premium iPad craft is not a v1 goal. watchOS is deferred.
