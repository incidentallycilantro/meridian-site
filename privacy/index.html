Privacy Policy

Contact: meridiancalendar@proton.me · Support

Summary

Meridian is a calendar app that reads and writes your events through Apple’s EventKit on your device. Your schedule content stays on your device and in the calendar accounts you already use. Meridian does not sell personal data and does not use advertising identifiers.

Data Meridian accesses on device

With your permission, Meridian may access:

Permission Why
Calendars Show, create, edit, and delete events you choose
Reminders Task Inbox and creating tasks from Parse (optional)
Location (When In Use) Local weather whisper; optional home city without location
Notifications Optional event / prep / sync alerts
Microphone / Speech Speak to Parse only while you are speaking
Camera Optional flyer/invite scan into Parse
Photos (system picker) Optional image pick for OCR — Meridian does not request full Photos library access
iCloud / CloudKit Horizon Share invite/accept when you use sharing

Meridian does not present authoritative leave-by / drive-time claims in Alpha 1. Directions hand off to Apple Maps when an event has a place.

Data that may leave the device

Help Improve Meridian (default OFF)

If you turn on Settings → Privacy → Help Improve Meridian, Meridian may upload:

  • anonymous install id (UUID — not Apple ID, name, or email)
  • app version and iOS version
  • allowlisted feature counter codes and counts only

Never uploaded via Improve: event titles, notes, locations, attendees, calendar names, meeting URLs, search text, or raw EventKit content.

Turning Improve off stops uploads and requests deletion of remote Improve rows for that install id.

System services

  • WeatherKit — weather for your location or home city when weather is shown
  • MapKit / Apple Maps — geocoding / directions when you open Directions
  • CloudKit — only for Horizon Share flows you initiate
  • MetricKit — Apple diagnostic payloads processed on device; Meridian logs opaque codes, not calendar content
  • Apple notifications — local notification copy without calendar notes in bodies

Tracking

Meridian does not track users for advertising. No App Tracking Transparency prompt. Improve is not used for ads.

Widgets and Live Activities

The widget extension shares a limited App Group snapshot for glanceable schedule UI. Live Activities show active-event phase cues when enabled. These stay on device / system surfaces.

Your choices

  • Deny any permission — core schedule still works where optional features are denied
  • Choose which calendars appear in Meridian
  • Keep Help Improve off (default)
  • Delete Improve remote data from Privacy settings

Children

Meridian is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

Material privacy changes will update this policy and, where required, App Store privacy labels.
